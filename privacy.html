Privacy policy

Fit Marty is a private app used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission, the app reads the owner's own fitness data from Google (activity, steps, heart rate, sleep and calories). That data is stored only on the owner's personal computer, is used only to show the owner their own health and fitness information, and is never sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings, under third-party connections.
